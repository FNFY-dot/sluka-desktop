When Sluka Desktop puts this add-on into Anki, it puts beside it, in
its web folder, two of the app's own files, shim.js and anki-page.js,
and in its ext folder a link to (or a copy of) the Sluka extension's
own files.  Those are Sluka's, and stay under Sluka's terms
(https://sluka.app/terms): they are the Sluka extension's code, which
runs in Anki's pages as it runs in a browser, and talks with this
add-on only through the messages Anki's pages send it (pycmd).
